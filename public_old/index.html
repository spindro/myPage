Biography

I am a PhD candidate at the ISPAMM lab of Sapienza University of Rome. I’m working under the supervision of Dr. Simone Scardapane and Aurelio Uncini. My main interest is trustworthy machine learning for graph structered data. More in general anything that combines neural networks and graphs grabs my attention. Previously I was an intern at the Alcor Lab where my research focused on Computer Vision and Robotics (SLAM).
