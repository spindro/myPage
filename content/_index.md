I am an __Assistant Professor__ (rtd-A) at Sapienza University of Rome and a member of the [ELLIS Society](https://ellis.eu/). My research focuses on automating data science and robotic learning, a goal I am actively pursuing with the [PINlab](https://www.pinlab.org/) led by Prof. [Galasso](https://fgalasso.bitbucket.io/). Our mission is to advance robotic capabilities by integrating __Generative AI__ to create synthetic humans, enabling safe interaction in simulated environments before deployment in the real world. I hold a PhD in ICT, supervised by Prof. [Scardapane](https://www.sscardapane.it/), where I specialized in __trustworthy graph neural networks__. I am open to research collaborations and thesis supervision opportunities.
