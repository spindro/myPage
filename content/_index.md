I am an __Tenure Track Assistant Professor__ at Sapienza University of Rome and a member of the [PINlab](https://www.pinlab.org/) and [ELLIS Society](https://ellis.eu/). My research centers on AI agents built with VLMs and LLMs, focusing on their safety, collaboration, and memory across both real and digital worlds. This builds on my background bridging 3D/4D computer vision and robotics to reconstruct and re-simulate the dynamic world. I am an NVIDIA Academic Grant recipient and Principal Investigator of multiple research initiatives, including a Sapienza seed grant to found the startup [advAIse](https://advaise.net/), where I'm serving as Co-founder and CTO.
