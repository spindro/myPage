# Biography

I am a PhD candidate at the [ISPAMM lab](http://ispac.diet.uniroma1.it/) of Sapienza University of Rome. I'm working under the supervision of Dr. [Simone Scardapane](http://ispac.diet.uniroma1.it/scardapane/) and [Aurelio Uncini](http://www.uncini.com/). My main interest is trustworthy machine learning for graph structered data. More in general anything that combines neural networks and graphs grabs my attention. Previously I was an intern at the Alcor Lab where my research focused on Computer Vision and Robotics (SLAM).
